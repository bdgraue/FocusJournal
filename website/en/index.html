Features

How Focus Journal works

Every screen of the app, and what sits behind it. You can view the images light or dark — independently of how this page looks right now.

Overview

Your journal

All entries sit below one another, grouped by day. Today and Yesterday are named; older days carry their full date. A star in the corner marks the entries that matter to you.

One tap on the plus and you write today's entry.

grouped by day · Today and Yesterday named

Writing

Write it, then let it go

You write your entry on the day it happened. You can edit or delete it on that day only. After that it stands.

That takes away the pressure to polish everything later. Reading old entries is of course always possible.

editable and deletable on the same day only

Calendar

The calendar

A dot under the date shows which days you wrote on. Tap a day and read what was going on back then.

You can see at a glance how often you showed up.

one dot per day with an entry

Search

Find everything again

Search for a word and the app shows every entry it appears in — the match is highlighted right in the text.

The search runs on your device. It needs no internet and sends nothing away.

full text across all entries · runs on the device

Highlights

Stars for good days

Mark special entries with a star. The app collects all your stars in one place, so you find the good days again without scrolling.

Not a fan of stars? Turn them off in the settings.

stars can be switched off in the settings

Security

Locked up safely

Protect your journal with a PIN, a password or a pattern. Fingerprint and face unlock work too. Put the phone down and the app locks itself. After five wrong tries you have to wait — a little longer each time.

The entries themselves sit encrypted with AES-256-GCM on the device, the key for them in your system's protected storage. And the app has no permission to reach the internet at all: there is no server anything could end up on.

PIN · password · pattern · biometrics — 5 wrong tries → 30 · 60 · 120 · 300 seconds wait

Backup

Back up and restore

Make a backup of your journal. It is encrypted with your password and saved as a .fjb file — where it goes is up to you.

When you restore it, you decide: replace everything, add only what is new, or merge intelligently. If the file has been altered, the app notices and refuses it — rather than quietly importing damaged entries.

AES-256-GCM · PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 · 100,000 rounds · .fjb format

Reminder

A small reminder

The app reminds you to write once a day, if you like. You pick the time. That turns a good intention into a habit.

The same settings hold your lock, your backups and the switch for the stars.

one reminder a day · time of your choosing

Open source

Check it instead of trusting it

The complete source code of Focus Journal is public. Anyone can read what the app does — and what it does not.

The strongest claim on this page is not in a marketing line but in a file you can open yourself: the Android manifest. It lists what the app asks of the operating system — and internet access is not among them.

What the app asks of the system

  • USE_BIOMETRICfingerprint and face unlock
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONSthe daily reminder
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMso it arrives at the time you set
  • USE_EXACT_ALARMthe same, for newer Android versions
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDrestores the reminder after a reboot
  • INTERNETnot requested — the app never reaches the network

Questions

Answered briefly

Do I need an account?

No. You open the app, set up your lock and start writing. There is no sign-up and no email address you would have to give.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes — always. On Android, Focus Journal does not even ask for permission to reach the internet. Writing, reading, searching, backing up: it all happens on your device.

Can I edit an entry later?

No. Editing and deleting only work on the day you wrote the entry. After that it stays as it is. You can read it forever.

What if I lose my device?

That is what backups are for. Make one regularly and keep it in a safe place. On the new device you restore it. The backup is encrypted with your password — remember it well, because without it nobody gets to those entries, including you.

Who can read my entries?

Only you. Entries sit encrypted on your device, the key for them in your system's protected storage. On top of that you protect the app with a PIN, a password, a pattern or your fingerprint. There is no server and nothing is passed on to third parties.

What does Focus Journal cost?

Nothing. The app is free and open source. There are no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. No data is collected or sold either — which would be hard anyway, since the app is not allowed online.

Nothing else stands in the way: get the app, set up your lock, write the first entry.
